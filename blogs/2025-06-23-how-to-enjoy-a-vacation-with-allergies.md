Summer brings the promise of freedom; family vacations, road trips, beach days, and spontaneous outdoor adventures. But for allergy sufferers, that freedom often comes with added concerns. Whether it’s food allergies, seasonal triggers like pollen, or the risk of insect stings, managing allergies while traveling can feel like a full-time job.
