Managing a severe allergic reaction shouldn’t be complicated. When anaphylaxis strikes, clarity and speed matter most. Nasdepi® is being developed as a needle-free epinephrine option that’s designed for simple, rapid intranasal use, especially helpful for people with needle phobia.
