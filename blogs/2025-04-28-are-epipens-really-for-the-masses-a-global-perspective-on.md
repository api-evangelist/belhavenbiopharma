The Life-Saving Role of Epinephrine For individuals with severe allergies, an EpiPen can mean the difference between life and death. These auto-injectors deliver epinephrine, a crucial medication that helps reverse the effects of anaphylaxis. However, despite their importance, accessibility challenges raise a critical question: Are EpiPens truly available for the masses?
